# Virtual Rounding — Demo Script (~4 min)

**Persona:** A care provider doing rounds across a hospital floor.

## Setup
- Open the Virtual Rounding app (code app play URL, or the Teams personal tab).
- Optionally resize the browser to tablet width.

## Flow

1. **Orient (rounding console).**
   "This is my rounding console. Rooms are grouped by hospital and unit, and I can see status and
   the current patient at a glance." Point out the header stats (Rooms / Occupied / Needs reset).

2. **Scan a unit.**
   Look at **Fabrikam Medical Center → 3 West**. Note **Room 301 (Jordan Rivera, Occupied,
   family invited)**, **Room 302 (Available)**, **Room 303 (Priya Nair, Occupied)**.

3. **Join a room.**
   On Room 301, click **Join** — it opens the room's Microsoft Teams meeting. "One click and I'm
   in the room's ongoing Teams meeting — no hunting for links."

4. **Handle a flagged room.**
   Go to **ICU → ICU 2 (Needs Reset)**. Click **Reset** to return it to Available (clears the
   patient). "When a patient is discharged, one tap resets the room."

5. **Family engagement.**
   Point to **Peds 201 (family invited · 3)**. "The solution tracks external family invites for
   the family/friends experience."

6. **Wrap.**
   "Everything runs on Dataverse with a modern code app — installable as an unmanaged solution,
   and it drops right into Microsoft Teams."

## Talking points
- Reimagined from SharePoint lists + canvas apps to **Dataverse + code app**.
- **Teams-ready** as a personal tab.
- **Installable & customizable** by any SE; ships with fictitious synthetic data.
